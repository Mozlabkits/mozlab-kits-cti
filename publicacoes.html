Teacher Workload and Teaching Quality in Rural Primary Schools in Mozambique: A Qualitative Study in Lichinga District

Author: Tavares Alberto Namalope

Year: 2026

Research location: Lichinga District, Niassa Province, Mozambique

This qualitative study examines the effects of teacher workload on teaching quality in rural primary schools in Mozambique.

The study used semi-structured interviews, non-participant observation and document analysis to investigate how workload, teacher shortages, administrative responsibilities and institutional conditions influence lesson preparation, classroom interaction, assessment and pedagogical innovation.

Publication: Zenodo

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22018010

Language: English
